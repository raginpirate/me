Universal Commerce Protocol

Open standard for agentic commerce, led by Google and Shopify. In since the first week.

Seven days after the repository opened I reorganized payments, services and capabilities into a single hierarchy, and that is still the shape of the spec. Since then I have authored three of its five payment extensions and the instrument model they stand on.

  • Split payments pay with more than one instrument in a checkout; open-amount and fixed-amount modes; business-declared allowed combinations.
  • Payment authentication 3-D Secure challenges and device-data collection as first-class payment actions, so an agent-driven checkout clears step-up instead of failing it.
  • Payment terms deposits, instalments and pay-later as a schema for when money moves, not only how much.
  • Credential model split PAN from network-token credentials and expressed instrument requirements as request constraints. A breaking change, shipped cleanly.
  • anyOf constraints so a business can say "card or wallet, but a card needs a CVC" in the schema itself.
  • Available instruments handlers declare what they can accept up front. See the W3C entry below for where that came from.

W3C Web Payments Working Group

Shopify's representative since August 2025, including TPAC 2025.

Secure Payment Confirmation, browser-bound keys, 3-D Secure step-up, Payment Request and Payment Handler. I review the SPC UX guidelines and sit in the Web Payment Security Interest Group alongside the card networks, the issuers and the browser vendors.

In September 2025 I asked the group how an agent would discover which payment methods a buyer actually has. Nobody had an answer. In February 2026 I shipped one in UCP. At TPAC I argued the privacy line for agentic payments: a merchant or an issuer should never see the conversation you had with your agent, unless a dispute makes it necessary.

FIDO Alliance

Where passkeys meet checkout.

Member work on passkey-backed payment credentials and on what an agent is allowed to prove on your behalf. The aim is biometric, browser-mediated checkout that proves the payer well enough that a 3-D Secure detour is no longer needed.

Shopify Payments

The rails under more than a tenth of US ecommerce.

The day job, and where the standards work above meets production. I started on Subscriptions, wiring PayPal, Stripe and Apple Pay into recurring billing on Rails and GraphQL. Now I work on the payments platform itself, which is the first place a spec I wrote has to survive real traffic.
